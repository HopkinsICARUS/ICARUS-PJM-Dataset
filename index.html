About ICARUS

ICARUS is a joint research initiative between the Ralph O'Connor Sustainable Energy Institute (ROSEI) at Johns Hopkins University, NSF Global Center EPICS, and the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory (APL), supported by a Johns Hopkins Discovery and Nexus Awards.

Our Mission

Make energy policy analysis accessible to all stakeholders.

States, NGOs, private sector, media organizations, universities, and other stakeholders often lack the technical expertise and computational resources to conduct rigorous energy policy analysis. ICARUS bridges this gap by providing expert analysis using our comprehensive PJM dataset and advanced modeling capabilities. In addition to our analyses, we provide recommendations for real-world policy implementation (if requested).

All analyses are provided free of charge. We welcome reasonable requests that align with our mission to support informed energy policy decisions.

For questions about this initiative, please contact Dr. Yury Dvorkin at ydvorki1@jhu.edu

How It Works

  1. Submit Your Problem Description: Fill out the form below describing your energy system or policy challenge. We appreciate detailed descriptions but understand in some cases it can be difficult to pinpoint specific issues. In such cases, describe the problem to the best of your ability and our team will follow up with questions to help clarify and narrow down the challenge.
  2. Expert Analysis: Our team analyzes your problem using the ICARUS-PJM dataset and modeling framework. When possible, please indicate your desired output format such as a brief, case study, policy recommendation, or other deliverable type.
  3. Receive Results: We deliver a concise brief with numerical results and actionable insights tailored to your timeframe.
  4. Implementation Strategy: Based on results and your interest, our team will prepare an implementation strategy to guide practical application of the findings.
Privacy Commitment: We do not publish submitted questions or any identifying information (names, emails, organizations).
