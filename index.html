About ICARUS

ICARUS is a joint research initiative between the Ralph O’Connor Sustainable Energy Institute (ROSEI) at Johns Hopkins University, NSF Global Center EPICS, and the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory (APL), supported by a Johns Hopkins Discovery Award.

Our Mission

Making energy policy analysis accessible to all stakeholders.

States, NGOs, companies, media organizations, universities, and other stakeholders often lack the technical expertise and computational resources needed to conduct rigorous energy policy analysis. ICARUS bridges this gap by providing expert analysis using our comprehensive PJM dataset and advanced modeling capabilities.

For questions about this initiative, contact Dr. Yury Dvorkin at ydvorki1@jhu.edu

How It Works

  1. Submit Your Question: Fill out the form below with your energy policy question and desired analysis outcomes.
  2. Expert Analysis: Our team analyzes your question using the ICARUS-PJM dataset and modeling framework.
  3. Receive Results: We deliver a concise brief with numerical results and actionable insights tailored to your timeframe.
Privacy Commitment: We do not publish submitted questions or any identifying information (names, emails, organizations). All submissions are kept strictly confidential.
